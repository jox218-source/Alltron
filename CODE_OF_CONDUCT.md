# Contributor Covenant Code of Conduct

## Our pledge

We want participation in the Alltron community to be welcoming and respectful, regardless of experience, identity, background, or point of view. We value clear, constructive discussion and make room for people learning in public.

## Expected behavior

- Be considerate, patient, and respectful.
- Critique ideas and code, not people.
- Explain disagreements calmly and accept that maintainers may set project scope.
- Respect privacy. Do not publish another person's personal information, credentials, household details, or private support material.
- Help keep technical discussions safe and accessible to newcomers.

## Unacceptable behavior

Harassment, insults, threats, discrimination, sexualized conduct, doxxing, deliberate disruption, and publishing private information without consent are not acceptable. Do not pressure contributors to share private logs, recordings, account access, or home network details.

## Enforcement

Project maintainers may remove or edit content, close discussions, or restrict participation when conduct violates this policy. Reports should include a link or enough context to locate the behavior. Maintainers will handle reports as discreetly as practical and will avoid sharing reporter details unnecessarily.

For conduct concerns, use GitHub's contact/reporting mechanisms for the repository or contact a maintainer through a channel listed on the repository profile. Do not use public issues for sensitive reports. This policy applies in project spaces and when someone is representing the project in public.

This code of conduct is adapted from the [Contributor Covenant, version 2.1](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/).
